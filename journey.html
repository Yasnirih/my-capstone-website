Journey Timeline

A timeline of the moments, experiences, and goals that shaped where I am today.

April of 2023

Application/Interview Process

Went through Accenture’s interview process and made it to the final round, where I was able to show my communication skills and interest in the apprenticeship program.

April 14th of 2023

Accepted Offer

Received the call that I've been given the official offer from Accenture and began preparing for the start of my apprenticeship.

October 2nd of 2023

Started Apprenticeship

Started my journey at Accenture as a Technology Youth Apprentice, beginning the program and getting introduced to the company, and program expectations as well as meeting everyone within my cohort.

October 2023 - January 2024

Foundational Training & Tech School

Completed training through Creative Classroom which went over MS365 skills and communication skills amongst other things and tech school, which assisted in building a foundation in technical skills, problem-solving, and professional communication.

February 2024

First Project Staffing

Worked closely with my first supervisor, Tina, during my first project staffing, where I gained early exposure to Accenture’s work culture and built foundational skills in coordination and communication.

May 10th of 2024

Take your kid to work day

Helped support and coordinate my supervisor at the time with Accenture's Take Your Kid to Work Day, which was one of the first larger events I was involved in at Accenture.

October 16th of 2024

C24 Onboarding Support (20+ Apprentices)

Worked with the core team to help onboard 20+ new apprentices, supporting communication, coordination, and making sure everything ran smoothly.

November 11th of 2024

Communications Coach (YAP Program)

Served as the first and only apprentice Communications Coach, supporting and mentoring apprentices within the cohorts below by providing feedback on written communication and overall delivery to help them build confidence and improve their professional writing skills.

December 3rd of 2024

Careerwise Panel

Was handpicked to be one of two youth apprentices to speak on a CareerWise panel, where I shared my experience in the program. This was one of the first major panels I was invited to participate in, with both the CEO and President of CareerWise in attendance, helping me build confidence in speaking and representing the apprenticeship program as well as myself.

June 24th of 2025

High School Graduation

Graduated high school while continuing to grow professionally at Accenture.

July of 2025

Transition from Technology to Marketing

Made the decision to switch from Technology to Marketing & Communications to better align with my strengths and interests.

Julyof 2025

Transition to Full-Time Role

Started working full-time at Accenture and got to fully dive into M + C work, I was excited to take on new challenges and continue growing in my career at Accenture.

August 26th of 2025

Started Hunter College

Began attending Hunter College while working full-time, balancing school and work at the same time.

August of 2025

Current Role – Canada M+C Team

Now working with the Canada Marketing & Communications team alongside Most Jahan, supporting projects related to events, communications, and internal initiatives.

2025-2026

Continued Panels + Core Team Support

Continued participating in panels and supporting the core team, taking on a bigger role in helping others and contributing to program initiatives.

May 15th of 2026

2026

Continuing into my second year of college while staying committed to growing professionally at Accenture.
